Android setup

  1. Download the APK on the Android phone you plan to use.
  2. Open the file and approve installation from that source when Android asks.
  3. Sign in with the account sent to you. The first successful sign-in links it to that phone.
  4. Tap Connect and approve Android's VPN prompt. OrbitVPN chooses an available location automatically.

Windows setup

  1. Download and extract the Windows ZIP.
  2. Open OrbitVPN.exe and approve the administrator prompt needed for its VPN adapter.
  3. Sign in, leave Locations on Auto, and press Connect.
  4. Minimize it to the notification area while using the VPN. Closing OrbitVPN disconnects it.
Signing in on another device signs out the old one on a one-device account. If you change devices and cannot sign in, send me a message so I can reset it.

What we keep

OrbitVPN keeps the information needed to run your account: the device linked to it, its expiry, total upload and download use, the selected country, and basic connection errors.

It does not intentionally record the websites you visit or the contents of your traffic. VPN traffic passes through the selected OrbitVPN server and its hosting provider.
