What actually matters when choosing a China VPN

Recent in-country evidence

A generic speed test from Europe or North America says very little about whether the same app connects from mainland China.

Your actual platforms

A strong iPhone recommendation is not useful if you need Windows and Android. Confirm every device before paying.

More than one route

A service needs alternatives when one exit, transport, or network path becomes unreliable. One good speed-test result is not enough.

A reachable support path

Download pages and ordinary email may be hard to reach after arrival. Save installers and support details in advance.

Clear device and data rules

Check simultaneous-device limits, monthly data allowance, renewal, and what happens when you replace a phone.

Clear limits and troubleshooting

No provider can promise permanent access on every Chinese network. Look for useful failure guidance instead of absolute claims.

What to do before you travel

  1. Install the app on every supported device. Do not assume you can reach the provider’s download page after arrival.
  2. Save an offline copy of the installer. Keep the Windows ZIP or Android APK somewhere you can access without the VPN.
  3. Sign in and connect before leaving. Verify that the account, device limit, and VPN permission flow are understood.
  4. Test the tasks that matter. Try ordinary browsing, messaging, calls, school portals, and any work tools you actually need.
  5. Record the support route. Save a contact that remains useful if the normal website is unavailable.
  6. Check current rules. China requires VPN services to be licensed. Read current official travel advice and follow local law.
